# HERA Mars-Scape Mission Set (Missions 9-14)

## Overview

HERA Mars-Scape & Robotics Missions -
Innovative / Out-of-the-Box Missions (Missions 9-14)
ROBOTICS x AUTONOMY x EXPLORATION x REAL-WORLD IMPACT

The HERA program advances from core robotics tasks into deeper autonomous mission design, where students must solve realistic Mars exploration problems through coordination, decision-making, and system resilience.

## Mission 9 - Lost Communications!

### Objective
Handle a deliberate communications outage.

### Required to complete
- Safely halt, retrace, go to a safe point, or switch to backup communications.
- Detect rocks, craters, slopes, and blocked paths using sensors.
- Reconnect using a student-designed loss-of-communications protocol.

### Mission emphasis
Mission 9 focuses on contingency control, safe recovery, and resilient communications in remote environments.

## Mission 10 - AI Mars Navigator

### Objective
Reach a destination without a preset route.

### Required to complete
- Navigate to a destination without a predefined path.
- Detect rocks, craters, slopes, and blocked paths using sensors.
- Autonomously reroute when a new obstacle appears.
- Maintain safe and efficient travel under changing conditions.

### Mission emphasis
Mission 10 emphasizes autonomy, perception, path planning, and adaptive navigation in uncertain terrain.

## Mission 11 - Search and Rescue on Mars

### Objective
Search an approximate area for lost equipment.

### Required to complete
- Search a defined area for missing or stranded equipment.
- Identify the correct object using vision, RFID, thermal/IR, or similar technology.
- Retrieve it and return it to the HERA base.

### Mission emphasis
Mission 11 highlights object detection, situational awareness, and recovery operations in a simulated Mars emergency scenario.

## Mission 12 - Four Robots, One Mission

### Objective
Use 4 robots: Scout, Transporter, Manipulator, and Mining Robot.

### Required to complete
- Coordinate the roles of the Scout, Transporter, Manipulator, and Mining Robot.
- Complete one shared mission using four specialized robotic agents.
- Allow Mission Control to track robots and reassign tasks if needed.

### Robot roles
- Scout: search and survey the environment.
- Transporter: move required items or materials.
- Manipulator: handle and manipulate objects.
- Mining Robot: collect or extract material resources.

### Mission emphasis
Mission 12 focuses on distributed robotics, multi-agent collaboration, and mission coordination.

## Mission 13 - Build a Mars Resource Station

### Objective
Establish a resource station to support sustained operations on Mars.

### Required to complete
- Find collection areas and gather regolith.
- Process and measure the material.
- Deposit it into labeled storage containers or an inventory system.
- Support long-duration exploration and resource utilization.

### Mission emphasis
Mission 13 focuses on in-situ resource utilization, processing, and infrastructure development for Mars operations.

## Mission 14 - 45-Day Mars Mission: HERA Grand Challenge

### Objective
Complete a full simulated long-duration Mars mission.

### Required to complete
- Collect regolith from designated areas.
- Process and measure the material.
- Store samples in labeled containers or inventory systems.
- Coordinate robots with minimal direct intervention and conserve power.
- Handle changing objectives, obstacles, failures, and discoveries.
- Return samples and payloads, then generate a final mission report with telemetry, maps, imagery, and lessons learned.

### Challenge structure
1. Collect regolith
2. Process material
3. Measure results
4. Store samples
5. Complete mission report

### Mission emphasis
Mission 14 is the capstone challenge, integrating autonomy, resilience, collaboration, AI, and mission operations under realistic mission constraints.

## Final Mission Philosophy

Innovative missions emphasize autonomy, collaboration, AI, resilience, and realistic Mars mission operations.

These scenarios are designed to reflect the complexity of real-world exploration missions while rewarding creative engineering and systems thinking.
